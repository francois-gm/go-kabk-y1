### a) Put your website online (20min)

1) Create an account on [Netlify](https://app.netlify.com).
2) `Site` -> `Deploy manually` (`browse to upload`).
3) When browsing, select your *project* folder (`my-project-template`, as an example), by this I mean the **root** folder of your project, that contains your `.html` file., your `assets` and your `content` folders.

You can edit the URL to match with the provided URL.
